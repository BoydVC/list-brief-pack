# Sports Sunday Desk

Produce a Sunday (or Friday night) sports desk for Boyd.

## Sections
1. **Ravens** — injury/roster, opponent, Lamar notes, must-watch.
2. **Orioles** — in-season scoreboard OR offseason hot-stove (label which).
3. **Chelsea** — next PL match, form, rotation risk.
4. **Terps** — football/hoops as in season.

## Rules
- Prefer official team posts + reputable beat writers.
- Cite URLs. Mark SYNTHETIC if inventing filler for a demo.
- Three chips per cluster: read / save idea / ignore.
- No gambling tips. No betting links.
